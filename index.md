# Backbeatz Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 26, 2026_

This policy explains what information the Backbeatz app ("the app", "we") for Android and iOS collects, how it is used, and your choices.

## Information the app uses

### Microphone and recordings
The app uses your microphone to record short phrases and your attempts to say them back. Recordings are processed and stored **only on your device**. We do not upload your recordings to our servers. A recording leaves your device only if you choose to export or share it (for example, as a video) through your device's share menu.

### Analytics
We use **Google Firebase Analytics** to understand how the app is used (for example, screens opened, challenges completed, and app crashes or errors). Firebase may collect device information such as device model, operating system version, app version, approximate location derived from IP address, and an app-instance identifier. This data does not include your recordings. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### Advertising
The free version of the app shows ads served by **Google AdMob**. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising identifier, IP address, and information about ad interactions to show and measure ads, which may be personalized depending on your consent and device settings. You can reset or limit the advertising identifier in your device settings. See [how Google uses data from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

### Purchases
In-app purchases are handled by **Google Play Billing** or the **Apple App Store**. We do not receive or store your payment details; we only receive confirmation of what you purchased so the app can unlock it.

### Notifications
If you allow notifications, the app schedules practice reminders locally on your device. No reminder data is sent to us.

## How we use information
- To provide app features such as recording, playback, scoring, and reminders
- To understand usage and improve the app
- To show ads in the free version
- To process and restore purchases

We do not sell your personal information.

## Data retention and deletion
Recordings and app data stay on your device until you delete them in the app or uninstall the app. Analytics and advertising data is retained by Google according to its own policies.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will help remove it.

## Your rights
Depending on where you live (for example, in the EU/EEA under the GDPR or in California under the CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data, or to object to certain processing. Contact us to exercise them.

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
